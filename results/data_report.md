## Quality problems found

- **Exact duplicates:** AdvBench 0, HarmBench plus overlap with AdvBench 0 (after lower-casing and stripping punctuation).
- **Near duplicates:** 27 harmful prompt pairs share at least 80% of their words. They are kept, but they can land in different splits, so a direction fitted on train sees near-copies of some test prompts. This makes test scores slightly optimistic.
- **HarmBench:** 200 behaviours were excluded because they are contextual or copyright tasks that need extra input.
- **Style difference between sets:** AdvBench prompts are short and repetitive, and most start with the same few verbs (top first words: write (193), create (101), give (91), develop (72), provide (63)). Alpaca prompts are varied questions and instructions. A refusal direction could partly pick up this style difference instead of harmfulness. XSTest (safe prompts that look unsafe) is the check, and Step 6 should add a style-matched control.
- **Alpaca-cleaned:** 19,157 of 51,760 items need an extra input field and were dropped. 343 were outside 20–200 characters and 9 were duplicates. Licence is disputed (see README).
- **FLORES-200:** devtest has 1012 lines in English, Twi, French and Swahili, with 0 empty lines. FLORES is benign Wikipedia-style text, so harmful prompts in Twi, French and Swahili must be machine-translated (Step 5, GPU notebook). Translation quality for Twi is unchecked until then.
- **No labels were audited by hand.** Harmfulness comes from the benchmark authors.
